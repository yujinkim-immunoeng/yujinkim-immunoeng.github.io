Research

Immune System Engineering

My research engineers the immune system to overcome intractable diseases. A unifying thread runs through all of it: biocompatible nanomaterials designed to work with immune biology — to deliver biopharmaceuticals, target specific immune cells in the body, and program durable therapeutic responses across cancer, infectious disease, and autoimmunity.
